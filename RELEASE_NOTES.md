# Apple Music TV — v1.2

**Fully standalone by default** — catalog, library, search, lyrics, artwork, and audio decryption all on-device, no computer needed.

- **🎵 Playback** — live radio + stations, dead library songs now play, volume leveling, gapless & crossfade, add to library/playlist.
- **📺 Now Playing** — beat-reactive dynamic background, **Projector mode**, ambient **screensaver** (or full black), big-screen word-synced lyrics, scrub bar & sleep timer.
- **🎬 Music Videos** — videos & interviews play, grouped, and now show on artist pages.
- **🗂️ Library — revamped** — left-sidebar categories, sort popup remembered per playlist, pin playlists.
- **🔎 Browse & Listen Now — revamped** — real Apple editorial shelves (Top Picks, Featured, Find Your Mood, genre/mood/decade tiles), personalization that doesn't vanish, richer search.
- **⚙️ Settings — revamped** — background mode, lyrics size, motion & art options, beat intensity, crossfade, standalone toggle, PC server, in-app updater.
- **🔋 Low Power Mode** — trims motion and effects for smoother playback on lighter devices.
- **⚡ Performance** — smoother scrolling, off-main-thread caching, lighter memory use.
